Five award categories

📊

Data Wizard

Powering Personalized Learning Award

For the educator who uses data to tailor instruction so every student's needs are met with precision — like turning reading assessment data into differentiated reading groups.

🧭

Tech Trailblazers

Everyday Excellence in Innovation Award

For seamlessly weaving technology into daily routines — like a series of virtual field trips that let students explore historical sites as part of the curriculum.

💡

Young Entrepreneurs

Digital Innovators Award

For the classroom that builds entrepreneurial spirit through creative digital projects — like students designing digital posters and marketing a school fundraiser.

🤖

AI Innovators

Classroom of the Future Award

For students who use AI to create, explore, and solve real problems — like generating interactive study guides with real-time feedback and personalized pathways.

🎨

Digital Artist

Digital Arts Masterpiece Award

For outstanding student digital art and music — like kindergarteners composing custom theme songs for their class transitions.

Ask about Tech Disruptors → Open to educators at any school. Ask project leader Natalia Morales for the guidebook and rubric.
New · Student track

Flock Innovators Challenge

Creativity. Technology. Real impact. Join the Flock!

A school club where students ages 9 and up investigate a real need at their school and design a solution using technology. Students research, build a prototype, and present their idea — the strongest proposals advance to a final presentation before a panel of judges.

💡

1. Research

Identify a real need in their school, then research it and consult their community.

🔍

2. Design

Develop a technology-based solution, then create, test, and improve a prototype.

🎬

3. Present

Submit the project, a portfolio, and a presentation video documenting the work.

🏆

4. Compete

Finalists present to a panel of judges and the winning project is announced.

What students can build

Digital citizenship & online safety Robotics, coding & artificial intelligence Podcasts, student news & student voice Sustainability, inclusion & school well-being
🏆

Grand prize: a sponsored educational experience in Orlando, Florida

The winning project earns round-trip airfare, hotel accommodations, admission to Universal Orlando Resort, and a visit to Telemundo Orlando.
