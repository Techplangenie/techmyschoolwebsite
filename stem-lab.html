Our newest venture

The Mobile STEM Lab.

TMS comes to you! Our van brings hands-on, project-based STEM classes to homeschool families and public schools across Puerto Rico — we build a full classroom wherever you are, then break it down and roll to the next site.

🚐 We come to you 🌎 Bilingual (EN/ES) 👩‍🏫 2 educators per class 🧒 8–12 students per session 🎟️ First class FREE 🤖 Robotics · Coding · Science · Math · Engineering

Why the van?

Every student on the island deserves to be served.

The homeschooling community matters too — the Mobile STEM Lab extends world-class learning opportunities to every type of student, regardless of circumstance.

🎯

Reach every demographic

With a homeschooling option, Tech My School now serves every type of student in Puerto Rico.

A rich learning experience

Experiential, dynamic, and cross-curricular — real inquiry, real collaboration, real experiments.

💚

Robotics · Coding · Science · Math · Engineering — 60-minute classes with 2 qualified teachers and 8–12 students per session. The van rolls in, the classroom pops up.

Every homeschool session is followed by a free public school session

After each pop-up, the van heads to a nearby public school and runs the same class at no cost — same educators, same equipment, same curriculum. It's part of our commitment to the island: when one community learns, the next one does too.

The gear

A full STEM classroom on wheels.

The van is off-grid capable — solar powered with Starlink internet — and carries tables, a canopy, and a full portable classroom. Classes pop up in parks, homes, and corporate spaces.
