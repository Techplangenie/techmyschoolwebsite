About us

Educators first. Nonprofit always.

Twenty years of experience across four continents, now devoted to giving every child a world-class education.

Our story

A sabbatical that became a movement.

Robert and Irina Cobbs founded Tech My School in 2021 after spending twenty years working in schools across four continents and every financial demographic. What began as a first sabbatical in Puerto Rico became something bigger.

They saw that Puerto Rican schools significantly lagged behind 21st-century educational practices — not for lack of talent or will, but budget. Rather than merely donating resources, they built a structured support system: comprehensive strategic plans, trained staff, and students gaining standards-aligned 21st-century skills.

Unlike typical EdTech companies, Tech My School is a true nonprofit. Donor contributions and partner funding fuel operations, with all revenue directed toward serving low-income schools at no cost — over $1 million in goods and services donated so far.

Our mascot, the Puerto Rican Green Parrot, symbolizes intelligence, hope, and our commitment to the island. 🦜

Mission, vision & values

Why we do this.

Our mission

To create a more equitable learning experience for all students by better leveraging technology in schools.

Our vision

To create a highly skilled next generation of empowered learners, innovators, and digital citizens.

Our philosophy

Every student has the capacity to learn, thrive, and become a lifelong learner. Technology, done right, transforms classrooms in five ways: it makes learning engaging, personalizes it to every student, powers collaboration, builds future-ready skills, and connects students with people, places, and ideas around the globe.

FAQs

Common questions.

What is Tech My School?
Tech My School is an educational nonprofit that serves schools by providing technology goods and services.
What types of services does Tech My School provide?
We create technology plans, conduct audits, deliver professional development, provide discounted EdTech tools, donate technology, do strategic planning with leadership, and offer extracurricular student opportunities like our esports league.
Who does Tech My School serve?
We modernize schools globally, with a primary focus on underserved schools in Puerto Rico at no cost. We also serve well-resourced schools whose payments subsidize services to schools in need.
How do I get started?
All you have to do is reach out — email info@techmyschool.org or book a meeting, and we'll begin the process of modernizing and maximizing technology at your school.
