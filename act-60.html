Act 60

Who we are

Tech My School Inc., known as Tech My School, is a U.S. 501(c)(3) education nonprofit based in Puerto Rico. Robert Cobbs (founder and president, CEO, and board member) and Irina Cobbs (COO and co-founder) founded the organization in 2021. The IRS issued its federal tax exemption in March 2022 (ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer).

Mailing address: P.O. Box 1986, Luquillo, PR 00773. Phone: (858) 899-7787. Email: info@techmyschool.org. Website: techmyschool.org.

Programs include individualized technology plans, teacher professional development, technology donations, the Mobile STEM Lab, and the Spring Educators EdTech Conference.

Official listing

Our CECFL listing

Tech My School is a U.S. 501(c)(3) organization and is listed as item 376 on the official 2026 CECFL list. The list names Tech My School Inc. and covers donations made from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

Donor FAQ

What is Tech My School?
Tech My School is a Puerto Rico-based 501(c)(3) education nonprofit, founded in 2021, that builds individualized technology plans, trains teachers, and donates technology so schools can offer a more equitable learning experience.
What is your EIN?
66-0996264.
Are you on the official CECFL list?
Yes. Tech My School Inc. is item 376 on the official 2026 CECFL list of organizations qualified in connection with Section 6020.10 of Act 60-2019, valid for donations made January 1 through December 31, 2026.
Where are you located?
Mailing address: P.O. Box 1986, Luquillo, PR 00773. The organization serves schools across Puerto Rico.
How do I donate?
You can give online via PayPal or Givebutter, or mail a check to P.O. Box 1986, Luquillo, PR 00773. For other arrangements, email info@techmyschool.org or call (858) 899-7787.
