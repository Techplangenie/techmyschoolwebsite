Our flagship

A Technology Plan for your school

Creating your school's Technology Plan is our flagship product. We've created hundreds of strategic plans for schools across Puerto Rico and the world.

Our process begins with our essential partner, Tech Plan Genie — the AI-powered planning platform and exclusive template source for all Tech My School services. From there, the ITP becomes a complete audit of your school through the lens of technology, bringing vision, clarity, and meaningful growth to your technology program.
