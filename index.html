Our mission

A more equitable learning experience for all students.

Founded in 2021 by educators with twenty years of experience across four continents, Tech My School is a true nonprofit: every dollar of revenue goes toward serving low-income schools at no cost to them.

We don't just donate devices — we build strategic plans, train staff, and make sure students gain standards-aligned 21st-century skills. We teach schools how to fish.

Why we do it

Education is the foundation of everything.

Every industry — medicine, engineering, agriculture, the arts — is built on the classrooms that came first. Education shapes an economy's future, and in Puerto Rico the data shows a system in urgent need of support.

📉
92 Points Behind
Puerto Rico vs. the U.S. mainland

On the international PISA exam, Puerto Rico's students scored 378 in math to the mainland's 470 — a gap of several grade levels, with reading and science trailing similarly.

🌍
Below Mexico
And every OECD country measured

Puerto Rico placed last among the 35+ school systems in the OECD's published comparison, 30 points behind Mexico.

🇺🇸
Last in America
Lowest-scoring U.S. jurisdiction

Home to 3.2 million U.S. citizens, Puerto Rico records the lowest public-education outcomes of any state or territory.

Source: OECD Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA), 2015 results.

These are American children, and they deserve the same opportunity as any student on the mainland. Closing that gap is why Tech My School exists.
