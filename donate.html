The problem

Puerto Rico's education is one of the lowest performers in the world.

Puerto Rico's students scored 378 in math on the international PISA exam — 92 points below the U.S. mainland, below Mexico, dead last in the OECD's comparison. The weakest education in America, on American soil. Education is the foundation every industry stands on — and improving it has never been more needed than now.

Puerto Rico Act 60

Turn your tax incentive into generational impact.

Tech My School is 501(c)(3), §1101.01(a)(2), and CECFL certified (the Eradicating Child Poverty List) — which means your entire $10,000 annual Act 60 requirement can go to us, and we are eligible to receive the full amount.

What is Act 60?

Enacted July 1, 2019, consolidating Acts 20 & 22 into a unified tax code, Act 60 attracts individuals and businesses to Puerto Rico with significant tax incentives — including 0% income tax on PR-sourced interest and dividends for individual investors and 2–4% corporate rates for export service companies.

The charitable requirement

  • Donate at least $10,000 annually to approved nonprofits — Tech My School qualifies for the full $10,000, including the half reserved for CECFL-listed organizations
  • Maintain 183+ days/year presence in Puerto Rico
  • Purchase a primary residence within two years

Contribute $10,000 or more to Tech My School in the tax year.

Retain your receipts — we provide formal year-end reporting for your records.

Include the donation in your Act 60 filings. Done — and a school's future is transformed.
